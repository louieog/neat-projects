Platform reality check

What Apple's published APIs do—and do not—establish.

Messages framework ≠ inbox API Verified

Apple's public Messages framework is for iMessage app extensions, stickers, media and interactive message experiences. It does not document a general-purpose API for reading all personal conversations. Apple docs ↗

Policy and uptime are product risks Verified rule

App Store guideline 2.5.1 requires public APIs for apps distributed through the store. That does not, by itself, establish the legality or reliability of every external relay configuration. Guidelines ↗

A “phone farm” is a plausible industry implementation pattern, not a confirmed claim about Instinct. Even with dedicated hardware, delivery reliability, account integrity, consent and operational support remain open questions.

Execution strategy / Founder playbook

Ship the smallest truth.

A focused, safety-first MVP can test whether people pay for completed outcomes before building general-purpose autonomy or proprietary messaging infrastructure.

12-week illustrative build plan

Assumes a small experienced team; schedule is an estimate, not a commitment.

Weeks 1–2

Prove the job

Pick one task vertical. Interview target users. Run a concierge pilot. Instrument completion, time saved and user trust.

Weeks 3–5

Build the agent core

Messaging gateway, auth, task queue, isolated browser, action logs, memory boundaries and human approvals.

Weeks 6–8

Harden workflows

Retries, timeouts, tool permissioning, failure handoffs, secrets vault and end-to-end task evaluations.

Weeks 9–12

Paid beta

Subscriptions, cohort analytics, unit-cost tracing, incident runbooks and narrow expansion to adjacent tasks.

Non-negotiable launch gates

Approval before impact

Require confirmation for purchases, outbound messages, destructive edits and sensitive account actions. Log what the assistant plans to do and what it actually did.

Real-world reliability

Measure task success, human escalation, retries, time-to-resolution and costs per completed task—not just chat engagement.

Data boundaries

Collect the minimum necessary data; encrypt secrets; provide deletion, access logs and revocable connections.

Channel fallback

Support SMS, web or email fallback so the business is not entirely dependent on a brittle iMessage integration.

Frequently misunderstood

Does Instinct need to read my entire iMessage history?

No. An assistant you text at its own identity can receive messages addressed to it without receiving your unrelated personal conversations. Access to your broader history is a separate, much more sensitive architecture.

Is Instinct confirmed to run a phone farm?

No. The public product surface does not prove a specific transport, fleet size, Apple account strategy or device architecture. The scale calculator models one possible implementation only.

Are the default costs factual market quotes?

No. They are transparent illustrative inputs to help you test sensitivity. Obtain actual quotes for messaging, hosting, LLMs, browser tools, support and payment processing before making an investment decision.

Does the model prove this business will be profitable?

No. It excludes engineering salaries, corporate overhead, taxes, refunds, churn dynamics beyond a simplified LTV ratio, customer acquisition uncertainty and many failure costs. It is a decision aid, not a forecast.
